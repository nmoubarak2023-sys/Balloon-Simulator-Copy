01 BOUNDARY MESH
Demonstration container

Import one closed mesh. Rhino: Mesh → Export Selected → .3dm. Radii and dimensions use your model units.

Choose ROTATE BOUNDARY, select X, Y, or Z, then enter degrees or use the 90° / 180° presets. Rings snap to 90° by default. Rotation resets unfrozen edits. Frozen geometry stays fixed; a rotation that would exclude it is rejected.

02 POPULATING POINTS
03 FINAL BALLOONS

Generation resets unfrozen edits, while frozen shapes, popped balloons, and merge groups persist. Seeds repeat within this app.

04 CURVE PRESSURE

Choose MOVE CURVE, then drag a pink line to move the entire curve. CURVE POINTS edits its shape. All curves share strength and influence; the nearest curve controls pressure.

Edit curve, then apply pressure. Inward compression retains a 25% core. Wall and contact constraints are reapplied. Applying pressure resets unfrozen edits. Frozen shapes remain fixed.

05 INDIVIDUAL BALLOON

Select a balloon in either canvas.

Scales one world axis about the balloon center. The other two dimensions and the uniform scale stay unchanged. Factor 1 resets this axis.

Moving or scaling a balloon dents unfrozen neighbors to make space on release. Walls and frozen balloons remain fixed. Moves cannot destroy or split a neighbor. Undo restores the complete field before a move.

06 POP! / FREEZE / MERGE

Pop! removes the active balloon and dents unfrozen neighbors. Set the pressure before popping, or adjust it after the last pop. A later edit, freeze, merge, or regeneration ends that live adjustment.

Freeze locks the active balloon's exact current shape and position. Frozen balloons remain fixed during curve pressure, regeneration, and nearby pops. Unfreeze before moving, popping, or merging them.

Shift/Ctrl-click in either canvas, or use the checkboxes. Merge joins nearby balloons into one connected volume, filling only small contact gaps and removing internal interfaces.

07 OUTPUT & EXPORT

Precision requires Start-Balloon-Precision.cmd on this computer. It retains the contact-gap setting and uses tolerance-scale numerical repairs.

Rebuild creates a new surface from the occupied volume. It avoids CSG triangle fans, but fine walls, gaps and sharp details are limited by the grid size. Original balloons remain unchanged.

Separate input shells are always preserved.

MOVE CLIP drags the plane; ROTATE CLIP turns its rings. Clipping does not trim exports.

        ROTATE BOUNDARY rotates the actual imported mesh around its center.
        Select X, Y, or Z and enter exact degrees or choose a 90° / 180° preset.
        The rings snap to 90° by default; Ring snap also offers 15°, 45°, or
        Free. Rotation regenerates the balloon field and resets individual
        edits. Curve-point drags also regenerate the field and reset individual
        edits. Use ORBIT, or drag empty space, to change only the camera.
      

      

        Pop! removes the active balloon; the slider controls the local dent on
        its neighbors. Adjust the last pop until you make another edit. Freeze
        locks the active balloon's shape and position; click Unfreeze to edit it
        again. Shift/Ctrl-click several balloons or use the merge checkboxes,
        then click Merge. Only touching or nearby balloons can form a connected
        merged volume. Small contact gaps are filled, and internal interfaces
        are removed. These states are included in saved projects. Reset
        Pop/Freeze/Merge starts a fresh field while keeping the current boundary
        and settings.
      

      

        UNDO or Ctrl+Z reverses the last modeling or control action, including
        all neighboring shapes changed by a movement. Up to 20 actions are
        available. Normal text editing keeps its own Ctrl+Z behavior inside
        input fields. Camera navigation, selection, downloads, and opening this
        help are not undo steps. Undo history lasts for this page session and is
        not saved in projects.
      

      

        This is geometric form finding, not a physical material simulation.
        Solid intersections and separating contact planes prevent overlapping
        balloon volumes. Crowded fields can create small or empty cells; these
        are reported.
      

      

        The browser solver reinterprets the Grasshopper workflow using a
        different random generator and a solid geometry kernel. It does not
        execute RhinoCommon or reproduce the definition exactly.
      

      

        Shell assembly preserves separate shells in one mesh. It is not a union
        or a single solid. Boolean output is checked for valid, closed, manifold
        topology. Failed checks show warnings but do not block mesh export when
        a Boolean result exists. Exporting does not repair the mesh. Models are
        processed locally.
      

      

        Helvetica Neue LT Pro is used when installed, with Helvetica Neue,
